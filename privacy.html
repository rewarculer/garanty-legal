Privacy Policy

Garanty for Android · Last updated 14 October 2026

Garanty keeps everything on your phone. There is no account, no server and no analytics. Nothing you enter — not a receipt, a photo, a name or a phone number — is ever sent to us, because there is nowhere for it to be sent.

This version sells nothing, so the app does not even open the store connection a paid version would need. No part of Garanty contacts a server, and no data about you reaches us or anyone else.

1. Who this policy is from

Garanty is an independently developed Android app, published on Google Play under the package name com.rewar.garanty. In this policy, “Garanty”, “we” and “us” mean the developer of that app. You can reach us at rewarsherwanir@gmail.com.

This policy explains what the app stores, where it stores it, and what leaves your device. It applies to the Garanty Android app and to nothing else.

2. What Garanty stores on your device

All of the following is written to Garanty’s private storage area on your phone, and stays there.

Your profile

  • Your name
  • Your age, which the app works out from the birth year you enter
  • Your gender
  • Your city

Garanty asks for these once, during setup, so that it can address you by name and fill in a receipt’s buyer details for you. They are never used for anything else.

Your receipts

  • Item name, store name and category
  • Purchase date, warranty length and the expiry date calculated from them
  • Price and currency
  • Your own notes
  • A photo of the receipt, if you add one
  • Buyer details saved with the receipt: name, mobile number, age, gender and city

App settings

  • Language, list or grid preference, and your chosen reminder time
  • Whether you have finished the introduction
  • How many receipts you have created against the free allowance

Android’s automatic backup is switched off for Garanty, so none of this is copied into your Google account backup. It exists on the one device you entered it on.

3. Photos, metadata and location

When you attach a receipt photo, Garanty resizes it to roughly 2000 pixels and saves it as a JPEG inside its own storage.

The photo’s metadata is deliberately removed before it is saved. Phone cameras write an EXIF block into every picture, and on most phones that block contains the GPS coordinates of where the photo was taken. Garanty strips that block — including the location — and keeps only the image itself, rotated the right way up.

Garanty does not request the location permission, does not use location services, and has no way to know where you are.

4. What Garanty never does

  • No account. There is nothing to sign up for and nothing to sign in to.
  • No analytics, crash reporting or tracking. The app contains no analytics SDK of any kind. We do not know how many receipts you have, which screens you open, or whether you opened the app at all.
  • No advertising. No ad SDK, no advertising identifier, no profiling.
  • No selling or sharing of data. We hold none of your data, so there is none to sell, share or disclose — to anyone.
  • No cloud sync or backup. Your receipts are not uploaded anywhere.
  • No access to the rest of your phone. Garanty does not read your contacts, messages, call history, or files other than the photo you choose for a receipt.

6. Payment, and the absence of it

Garanty is free. There is no subscription, no in-app purchase and no payment of any kind, so the app never asks for a card, an address or a billing detail, and no payment information about you exists for us to hold or lose.

The Google Play Billing library is still bundled inside the app, which is why the permission list mentions billing. In this release it is never started: no store client is created, no purchase is queried, and no connection to Google is opened on your behalf. If paid plans are introduced later, this policy will be updated before they ship.

7. Reminders

Reminders are composed and scheduled entirely on your device by Android itself. There is no push notification service, no message passes through a server, and the text of a reminder — which includes the item name — never leaves your phone.

8. Links that leave the app

This release does not open any external links from inside the app. If a future version does, you leave Garanty at that point and the destination’s own privacy policy applies.

9. Children

Garanty is a general-audience app for keeping track of your own purchases, and it is not directed at children under 13. It asks for an age during setup so that it can fill in a receipt’s buyer details; like everything else, that answer stays on the device and is never transmitted.

10. Keeping and deleting your data

Your data stays on the device until you remove it, and you are always in control of it:

  • Delete one receipt in the app, and its photo is deleted with it.
  • Uninstall Garanty, and everything it stored goes with it — profile, receipts and photos.

Because nothing was ever transmitted, uninstalling genuinely is the end of it. There is no copy on a server for us to delete afterwards.

One thing deletion does not undo: the count of receipts you have created against the free allowance. That count records receipts created, not receipts currently held, so deleting a receipt does not return a slot. See the Terms of Service.

11. Your rights

Privacy laws such as the GDPR and the CCPA give you the right to see, correct, export and erase the personal data a company holds about you.

We hold none. There is no database with your name in it and no profile of you anywhere. Those rights are exercised directly on your own device: everything Garanty knows about you is visible in the app, editable in the app, and deletable in the app. If you would like to ask about any of this, write to us at rewarsherwanir@gmail.com.

12. Security, honestly stated

Garanty’s data sits in its private storage area, which Android keeps separate from other apps, and which is covered by your phone’s own disk encryption when you use a screen lock.

That protection is Android’s, and it has limits worth knowing. On a rooted or compromised device, an app with root access can read any other app’s storage. A full device backup that you make yourself may contain Garanty’s data, and is then only as safe as wherever you put it. We cannot protect data on a device that has been unlocked, rooted, or handed to someone else.

If this policy changes, the revised version is posted on this page with a new date at the top. If a change is significant, it will also be noted in the app’s release notes on Google Play. Continuing to use Garanty after a change means you accept the revised policy.
